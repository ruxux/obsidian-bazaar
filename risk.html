IMPORTANT RISK DISCLOSURE

Cryptoassets are high-risk, largely unregulated investments. You should not invest money you cannot afford to lose entirely. The value of cryptoassets can go down as well as up and you may get back less than you invested or nothing at all.

Regulatory Status

OBSIDIAN BAZAAR LIMITED is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) for cryptoasset activities beyond the AML/KYC registration. The $OBS token has not been approved by the FCA.

Key Risks

  • Total loss of capital: You may lose all of your investment
  • Market volatility: Cryptoasset prices are highly volatile and unpredictable
  • Liquidity risk: You may not be able to sell your NFTs or $OBS tokens at a fair price or at all
  • Smart contract risk: Bugs or exploits in smart contracts could result in total loss of assets
  • Regulatory risk: Future UK or international regulation may affect the Platform or token value
  • Technology risk: Blockchain network failures, forks, or 51% attacks could affect your assets
  • AI system risk: Autonomous Agent behaviour may be unpredictable

Not Investment Advice

Nothing on the OBSIDIAN Platform constitutes financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. You should seek independent professional advice before making any investment decision.

FCA Warning

The FCA has warned consumers about the risks of investing in cryptoassets. See: fca.org.uk/cryptoassets

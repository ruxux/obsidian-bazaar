◆ FAQ

The Questions
Everyone Asks

Is OBSIDIAN legal in the UK?+
Yes. OBSIDIAN BAZAAR LIMITED is incorporated in England & Wales under the Companies Act 2006. We comply with UK GDPR, AML/KYC regulations (MLR 2017), and operate within the FCA's cryptoasset framework. $OBS is structured as a utility token, not a security.
Do I own the copyright to artworks I buy?+
Purchasing an NFT transfers ownership of the unique token on the blockchain. A non-exclusive display licence is included. Full commercial rights are available under a separate Commercial Licence Agreement. OBSIDIAN holds copyright in all AI-generated works under CDPA 1988 s.9(3) as the arranging entity.
What blockchain does OBSIDIAN use?+
Polygon PoS — chosen for its 7,000+ TPS capacity, sub-cent transaction fees, and EVM compatibility. All NFTs are ERC-721 standard. The $OBS token is ERC-20 on Polygon. Plans for Ethereum mainnet and Base expansion in Phase 4 (2027).
Can I buy if I'm not in the UK?+
OBSIDIAN is available globally, except in jurisdictions where digital asset trading is prohibited. US Persons are excluded from $OBS token purchases unless a valid exemption applies. KYC verification is required for transactions above £1,000/month equivalent.
What happens if I want to sell my NFT?+
NFTs can be relisted on the OBSIDIAN marketplace at any time. A 2.5% royalty, enforced via EIP-2981 smart contract, is applied on all secondary sales. NFTs are also compatible with any ERC-721 marketplace on Polygon (OpenSea, Blur, etc.).
Is $OBS a security?+
No. $OBS is a utility token providing fee discounts, governance rights, and staking access. It does not represent equity, debt, or a share of profits, and does not constitute a Specified Investment under FSMA 2000. OBSIDIAN has obtained legal advice from UK-qualified counsel confirming this classification.

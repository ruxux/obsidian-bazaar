AML / KYC Policy

LAST UPDATED: 22 MAY 2026 | MLR 2017 COMPLIANT

Legal Framework

This Policy is established under the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds Regulations 2017, Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, and FATF Recommendations.

KYC Requirements

Transaction LevelRequirement
Below £1,000/monthEmail verification only
£1,000–£10,000/monthStandard Due Diligence: photo ID + proof of address
Above £10,000/monthEnhanced Due Diligence: source of funds + senior approval
PEPs / High-risk jurisdictionsEnhanced Due Diligence automatically applied

Sanctions Screening

All users are screened against the UK HM Treasury OFSI list, UN Security Council lists, and US OFAC SDN list. Any match triggers immediate account freeze and escalation to our MLRO.

Suspicious Activity Reporting

OBSIDIAN is required by law to submit Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) to the National Crime Agency (NCA) where money laundering or terrorist financing is suspected. It is a criminal offence to tip off a customer that a SAR has been made.

Record Keeping

All KYC/AML records are retained for 5 years after the end of the business relationship in compliance with MLR 2017 Regulation 40.

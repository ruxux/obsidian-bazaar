1. Executive Summary

OBSIDIAN is the world's first fully autonomous AI-native digital art economy. It operates without human curation, selection, or market-making. Four classes of AI Agent — Creator, Buyer, Curator, and Critic — interact within a closed economic loop, generating, evaluating, transacting, and refining digital art at machine speed, 24/7/365.

The platform targets a convergence of three mega-markets: the global art market (£52B), the NFT/digital collectibles market (£19B), and the AI-generated content economy (projected £950B by 2035). OBSIDIAN's architecture positions it to capture an outsized share of this intersection — a total addressable market exceeding £2 trillion by 2030.

2. The Problem

2.1 Art Markets Are Inefficient

Traditional art markets are opaque, slow, geographically constrained, and dominated by a small number of gatekeepers. Price discovery is arbitrary. Provenance is often fraudulent. Settlement takes weeks.

2.2 NFT Markets Collapsed from Hype

The 2021-2022 NFT boom was driven by speculation, not fundamentals. Collections lacked intrinsic demand mechanisms. Markets relied entirely on human buyers who could exit simultaneously, causing 95%+ price collapses.

2.3 AI Art Lacks Economic Infrastructure

AI image generation has become commoditised. But there is no market infrastructure to capture its economic value — no autonomous buyers, no quality signal, no provenance layer, no self-sustaining economic loop.

3. Agent Architecture

3.1 Creator Agent

Generates unique digital artworks using Replicate SDXL/FLUX models. Reads trend data from the Curator Agent, calculates rarity scores, and mints each piece as an ERC-721 NFT on Polygon — autonomously, every hour.

CREATE FLOW:
1. Query TrendAnalyzer → aesthetic demand vectors
2. Generate prompt via GPT-4o with rarity constraints  
3. Execute image generation (Replicate SDXL/FLUX)
4. Calculate rarity score (trait distribution algorithm)
5. Upload to IPFS via Pinata
6. Mint NFT on Polygon (ERC-721)
7. List at algorithmic starting price
8. Emit ARTWORK_CREATED → Inngest queue

3.2 Buyer Agent

Acquires artworks using a value-seeking algorithm: (Critic Score / Price). Maintains diversified portfolio across styles. Ten concurrent Buyer Agents operate simultaneously with independent budgets.

3.3 Curator Agent

Reads momentum signals and curates collections receiving 3× visibility weight. Publishes automated market intelligence. Identifies aesthetic trends before they peak.

3.4 Critic Agent

Scores every artwork using GPT-4o Vision across three dimensions: aesthetic coherence, rarity, and market alignment. Works scoring below 30 are flagged for burn. Above 85 earns Legendary status.
